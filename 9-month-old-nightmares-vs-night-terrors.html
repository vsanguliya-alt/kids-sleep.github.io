Nightmares vs. Night Terrors in 9-Month-Olds: A Parent's Guide

For a 9-month-old, the concepts of nightmares and night terrors are generally not applicable. Very young babies do not have the cognitive development to experience these sleep disturbances in the same way as older children. Night terrors typically begin in toddlers around 18 months of age and are most common in children aged 3 to 7 years . They are not a typical occurrence in a 9-month-old.
