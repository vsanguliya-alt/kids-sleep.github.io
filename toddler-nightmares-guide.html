Toddler Nightmares in Children 0: Complete Parent Guide

Safe Sleep Basics

Follow AAP/NHS safe sleep guidelines: firm mattress, no loose bedding, room-sharing without bed-sharing.

When to Seek Help

Seek immediate medical care if symptoms worsen, persist beyond expected, or if your child has difficulty breathing, persistent fever, or dehydration.

Tips for Better Sleep

Ensure rest, hydration, and comfort. Follow evidence-based guidance from NIH/NHS.

Red Flags

Seek immediate care for severe symptoms, breathing difficulties, or if your child appears unusually lethargic.
