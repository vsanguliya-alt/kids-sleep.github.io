Screen Time for Children Before Bed: A Parent&#x27;s Guide

Establishing healthy screen habits for children, especially before bedtime, is crucial for their sleep and overall well-being. The available information focuses on general sleep hygiene practices, which are applicable to establishing healthy routines for the whole family.

📱 Why Screens Before Bed Are Disruptive

The research consistently links screen use before bed to poorer sleep quality for children and adults. The reasons for this are twofold. First, the blue light emitted from screens can suppress melatonin production, the hormone that regulates sleep-wake cycles, making it harder to fall asleep. Second, the interactive and often fast-paced content is mentally stimulating, which is the opposite of the calm state needed for sleep.
