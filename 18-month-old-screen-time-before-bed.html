18-Month-Old Screen Time Before Bed: A Parent's Guide

Managing screen time for an 18-month-old can be tricky. The American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) recommends no screen time other than video chatting for babies under 18 months. For toddlers 18 to 24 months, the guideline shifts to allowing limited, high-quality screen time, ideally watched with an adult present . This typically means no more than one hour per day of high-quality programming for children aged 2 to 5 years .

Why It Matters Before Bed

The advice to limit screens before bed is supported by research. A randomized clinical trial involving toddlers found that removing screen time in the hour before bedtime resulted in modest improvements in sleep quality, including better sleep efficiency and fewer night awakenings .

One of the primary concerns is the effect of blue light emitted by screens. This type of light can disrupt the body's production of melatonin, the hormone that regulates sleep-wake cycles . A sleep specialist at the Cleveland Clinic explains that bright blue lights can "significantly affect our normal melatonin production, which controls our circadian rhythm," potentially disrupting sleep schedules . While some recent studies have questioned whether blue light has the same melatonin-suppressing effect on young children as it does on adults , the broader body of evidence still points to screen content and timing as "causal disruptive factors" for sleep .

Practical Steps for Bedtime

Aim to turn off all screens at least one hour before your child's bedtime. Replace screen time with calming, screen-free activities like reading books, doing puzzles, or listening to soft music. A "bedtime box" filled with these activities has been shown to be an effective substitute . The AAP encourages families to be their child's "media mentor," teaching them how to use technology as a tool to connect and learn .

If a nightlight is needed, choose a red or amber-colored one. These colors are less likely to disrupt melatonin production compared to bright blue or white lights .
