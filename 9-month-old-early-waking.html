Early Waking in 9-Month-Olds: A Parent's Guide

Early morning waking is a common challenge for parents of infants. While it can be frustrating, it's often a normal part of development and can be managed with simple adjustments.

What Is Normal for a 9-Month-Old?

At 9 months, your baby's sleep is becoming more predictable. Most 9-month-olds need about 12 to 16 hours of sleep in a 24-hour period, which typically includes 2 naps during the day. An age-appropriate wake-up time for a baby is usually between 6:00 AM and 7:30 AM. Waking up before 6:00 AM is often considered "early waking." However, every child is different, and some naturally have an earlier internal clock.

Common Reasons for Early Waking

Several factors can cause a 9-month-old to wake too early:

Over-tiredness or Under-tiredness: If your baby is getting too much daytime sleep (very long or late naps), they may not have enough "sleep pressure" to stay asleep until morning. Conversely, an overtired baby can also wake up early due to higher levels of stress hormones.
