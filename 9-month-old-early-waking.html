Early Waking in 9-Month-Olds: A Parent&#x27;s Guide

Early morning waking is a common challenge for parents of infants. While it can be frustrating, it&#x27;s often a normal part of development and can be managed with simple adjustments.

What Is Normal for a 9-Month-Old?

At 9 months, your baby&#x27;s sleep is becoming more predictable. Most 9-month-olds need about 12 to 16 hours of sleep in a 24-hour period, which typically includes 2 naps during the day. An age-appropriate wake-up time for a baby is usually between 6:00 AM and 7:30 AM. Waking up before 6:00 AM is often considered &quot;early waking.&quot; However, every child is different, and some naturally have an earlier internal clock.

Common Reasons for Early Waking

Several factors can cause a 9-month-old to wake too early:

Over-tiredness or Under-tiredness: If your baby is getting too much daytime sleep (very long or late naps), they may not have enough &quot;sleep pressure&quot; to stay asleep until morning. Conversely, an overtired baby can also wake up early due to higher levels of stress hormones.
