Signs of Sleep Apnea in an 18-Month-Old

Pediatric sleep apnea is a condition where a child&#x27;s breathing pauses during sleep because the airway has become narrowed or partly blocked . While it can occur in children of any age, it is most commonly diagnosed between ages 2 and 8 . Recognizing the signs of sleep apnea in your 18-month-old is important because untreated sleep apnea can lead to problems with learning, behavior, and even high blood pressure .

Nighttime Signs of Sleep Apnea in Toddlers

Loud Snoring and Breathing Pauses

Loud snoring is one of the most common symptoms. This is caused by air squeezing through a narrowed airway . However, not every child who snores has sleep apnea. The key red flag is snoring accompanied by long silent pauses in breathing, followed by snorts, gasps, or choking sounds as the child starts breathing again .

Restless Sleep and Unusual Positions

Children with sleep apnea often have restless sleep, tossing and turning frequently . They may also sleep in unusual positions, such as with their head tilted back, neck extended, or even in a seated position, to try and keep their airway open .

Other Nighttime Symptoms

Mouth breathing while asleep Nighttime sweating Bedwetting, particularly if they have been dry for a period and then start wetting the bed again Sleepwalking or night terrors Daytime Signs and Symptoms

Poor sleep at night can cause significant difficulties during the day. Watch for these signs:

Irritability, moodiness, or aggressive behavior Hyperactivity and difficulty paying attention, which can sometimes be mistaken for ADHD Daytime sleepiness, such as falling asleep during short car rides or at unusual times Morning headaches Poor weight gain or growth Speaking with a nasal-sounding voice If your child has one or more of these symptoms, particularly persistent loud snoring with breathing pauses, consult your pediatrician. They can evaluate your child and, if needed, refer you to a sleep specialist for a sleep study (polysomnogram), which is the most accurate way to diagnose sleep apnea .
