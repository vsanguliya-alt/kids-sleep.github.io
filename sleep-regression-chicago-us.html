Sleep Regression in Children: A Chicago Parent's Guide

If your child, who was once sleeping through the night, is suddenly waking up frequently or fighting bedtime, you may be dealing with a sleep regression. This is a temporary phase where a child's sleep patterns become disrupted. The good news is that it's a normal part of development and typically lasts only a few weeks .
