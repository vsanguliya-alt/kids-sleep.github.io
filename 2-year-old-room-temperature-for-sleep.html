Ideal Room Temperature for Your 2-Year-Old&#x27;s Sleep

Maintaining a safe and comfortable room temperature is an important part of helping your toddler sleep well. The established safe sleep guidelines for babies under 12 months continue to be a sound and comfortable guideline for a 2-year-old as well.

The Recommended Room Temperature

For safe and comfortable sleep, experts recommend keeping your child&#x27;s bedroom at a temperature between 16°C and 20°C. This range is widely recommended by organizations like the NHS and The Lullaby Trust.

Why This Temperature Matters

The main reason for this guidance is to prevent overheating, which can disrupt sleep and cause discomfort.

Comfort: A room that is too warm can make your child restless, sweaty, and uncomfortable, leading to more night wakings.

Prevents heat-related illness: Young children are more vulnerable to heat-related illness as they are less able to regulate their body temperature.

Promotes safe sleep: A cool room is a safe room. Keeping the temperature in check is a simple way to ensure your child is comfortable during sleep. The official guidance from the AAP, which advises keeping the room at a comfortable temperature for an adult, is not just for babies; it applies to toddlers and older children too.

How to Manage the Temperature

Use a room thermometer: This is the most reliable way to know the temperature of your child&#x27;s room, rather than just guessing.

Dress your child appropriately for the room temperature: Use lightweight bedding and pajamas, and add or remove layers as needed.

Monitor for signs of being too hot: Feel the back of your child&#x27;s neck or their tummy. If they feel clammy or sweaty, they are too warm. Red cheeks and damp hair are also signs of overheating.

Adjust the thermostat or use a fan: Use fans or air conditioning to keep the room cool.

A Critical Note on Overheating and SIDS

While SIDS is a risk primarily for infants under one year old, the principle of avoiding overheating is a good one to follow for your 2-year-old. Overheating is a known risk factor for SIDS in babies, and it continues to be a factor that can disrupt sleep and cause discomfort.

Summary

The ideal room temperature for your 2-year-old is 16°C (60°F) . This is the same safe and comfortable range recommended for infants and adults. By keeping the room cool, dressing your child appropriately, and monitoring for signs of overheating, you can help them sleep safely and soundly.
