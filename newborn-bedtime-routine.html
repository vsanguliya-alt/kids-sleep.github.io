Newborn Bedtime Routine: A Guide for Peaceful Nights

Establishing a consistent bedtime routine is one of the most important things you can do for your newborn&#x27;s sleep and overall well-being. A predictable sequence of calming activities helps signal to your baby that it&#x27;s time to transition from the busyness of the day to a restful state for sleep.

Why a Routine Matters

A bedtime routine provides a sense of structure and security. It signals to your baby that the busy day is ending and it&#x27;s time to transition to sleep. This can help reduce anxiety and make the transition to sleep smoother. Routines are also important for helping your child learn to self-soothe, a critical skill for healthy sleep.
