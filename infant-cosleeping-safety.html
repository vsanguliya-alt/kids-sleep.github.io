Infant Bedtime Routine: A Guide for Peaceful Nights

Establishing a consistent bedtime routine is one of the most important things you can do for your baby&#x27;s sleep and overall well-being. A predictable sequence of calming activities helps signal to your baby that it&#x27;s time to transition from the busyness of the day to a restful state for sleep. The American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) recommends a consistent, calming bedtime routine for babies.

Why a Routine Matters

A bedtime routine provides a sense of structure and security. It signals to your baby that the busy day is ending and it&#x27;s time to transition to sleep. This can help reduce anxiety and make the transition to sleep smoother. Routines are also important for helping your child learn to self-soothe, a critical skill for healthy sleep.

Sample Bedtime Routine (30Minutes)

You can adapt this routine to fit your family&#x27;s needs. The important part is doing the same calming activities in the same order each night.

Start the Wind-Down (About 1 Hour Before Bed): Dim the lights and speak softly. Turn off all screens, including the TV and your mobile phone, as the blue light can interfere with sleep.

Bath Time: A warm bath is a classic and effective way to relax your baby.

Change into Night Clothes: Dress your baby in comfortable, breathable pajamas.

Final Feed and Cuddle: Offer a feed shortly before putting your baby down. This is a great opportunity for quiet cuddles.

Story Time or Lullaby: Read a short bedtime story or sing a quiet, soothing song.

Into the Cot Drowsy but Awake: Place your baby on their back in the cot while they are sleepy but still awake. This is one of the most important sleep skills; it helps your baby learn to self-soothe and fall asleep independently. If they learn to fall asleep on their own at the start of the night, they will be more likely to settle themselves back to sleep if they wake up later.

Sleep Environment and Safety

Room Temperature: Keep the room at a comfortable temperature for an adult (around 16°C) .

Safe Sleep: Always place your baby on their back to sleep on a firm, flat mattress. Keep the sleep area clear of soft objects, pillows, and loose bedding .
