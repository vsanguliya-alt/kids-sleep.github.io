Ideal Room Temperature for Your 4-Year-Old's Sleep

Creating a comfortable and safe sleep environment is key to helping your 4-year-old get the rest they need. Room temperature plays a major role in this.

The Recommended Temperature Range

The most important guideline, especially for younger children, is to keep the room at a temperature that is comfortable for a lightly dressed adult. This helps prevent overheating, which is a known risk factor for Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS).

In the UK and Australia, guidelines often suggest a range of 16°C to 20°C for infants. For a 4-year-old, a safe and comfortable temperature is generally around 18°C. This range ensures they are comfortable and can sleep soundly through the night, making it a safe target for preschoolers as well.

Practical Tips for Maintaining a Safe Sleep Temperature

Use a room thermometer: This is the most reliable way to check the temperature. Avoid relying on your own comfort level alone.

Dress appropriately for the temperature: Use lightweight, breathable sleepwear. Avoid heavy blankets or over-bundling, which can add unnecessary heat.

Check for signs of overheating: Feel your child’s chest or the back of their neck. If it feels clammy or sweaty, they are too warm and you should remove a layer of clothing.

Keep the sleep space clear: Remove thick quilts, duvets, stuffed toys, and electric blankets that can trap heat.
