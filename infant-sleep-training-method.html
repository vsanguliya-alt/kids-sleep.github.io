Infant Sleep Training: Safe Methods for Better Sleep

Sleep training is the process of helping babies learn to fall asleep independently and return to sleep on their own if they wake at night . The goal is to help your baby learn the foundational sleep skills that will serve them for life. Most experts agree that formal sleep training can begin around 4 to 5 months of age .

Common Sleep Training Methods

🪑 The Ferber Method (Graduated Extinction)

This method involves putting your baby to bed awake, leaving the room, and returning at progressively longer intervals to provide brief reassurance . For example, you might check at 3 minutes, then 5 minutes, then 10 minutes . The key is not to pick them up; offer verbal comfort only . Some babies benefit from timed check-ins, while others become more upset by them, so adjustments may be needed .
