Infant Dropping Naps: A Guide to Understanding the Transition

It can be a confusing and frustrating time when your baby, who used to nap like clockwork, suddenly starts fighting daytime sleep. However, this is a normal developmental phase. Understanding how and why babies drop naps can help you navigate this change with more confidence.

How Many Naps Should My Baby Take?

The number of naps a baby takes is closely tied to their age. Here is a general guide to the typical number of daytime naps a baby needs:

Newborns: This is a period of rapid change. Newborns may sleep up to 16hours a day in short sessions, with their daytime sleep broken into several naps .

By 3months: Many babies settle into a pattern of 3 to 4 naps a day .

By 6months: A common pattern is 2 to 3 naps a day .

By 12months: Most babies transition to taking just one nap a day .
