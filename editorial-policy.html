# Editorial Policy 1. **Source-first.** We only publish claims that trace to a public health guideline (NIH, NICE, CDC, WHO). Every article links to its source so readers can verify the claim. 2. **Fact-check guard.** Every factual statement cites its source inline (e.g. "Source: NIH | Fever"). 3. **No diagnosis or treatment.** We inform, we do not diagnose or prescribe. Nothing here is a substitute for a licensed clinician. 4. **Content generation.** Articles are generated from structured public health guidelines using automated tooling, then checked against the source guidelines before publication. 5. **Human medical review (required).** Before this site is monetized or promoted, a licensed physician must review and sign off on the content. Reviewer identity and credentials are published here once engaged. (Status: pending engagement of a medical reviewer.) 6. **Corrections.** If a source guideline updates, we update the article. Report errors via the contact link in the footer.
