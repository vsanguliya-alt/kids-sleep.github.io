9-Month-Old Sleep Schedule: A Guide for Parents

At 9 months old, your baby is likely settling into a more predictable sleep pattern. This is a significant milestone that can help both you and your baby get more rest.

How Much Sleep Does a 9-Month-Old Need?

Most 9-month-olds need about 10 to 12 hours of sleep at night and 2 to 3 hours of daytime sleep, totaling 11 to 14 hours in a 24-hour period . This is typically divided into two naps per day—one in the morning and one in the afternoon . By this age, most babies have dropped the third late-afternoon nap .

Sample Sleep Schedule

A typical schedule for a 9-month-old might look like this:

Time Activity

7:00 AM Wake up

9:00 AM – 10:30 AM Morning nap (1.5 hours)

1:30 PM – 3:00 PM Afternoon nap (1.5 hours)

7:00 PM Bedtime routine begins

7:30 PM Bedtime

Aim for wake windows (time awake between sleeps) of about 2.5 to 3.5 hours at this age . Avoid letting your baby nap after 3:30 PM, as this can make it harder for them to settle at bedtime .

Safe Sleep Essentials

Room Temperature: Keep the room at a comfortable temperature for an adult (around 16°C) .

Safe Sleep: Always place your baby on their back to sleep on a firm, flat mattress. Keep the sleep area clear of soft objects, pillows, and loose bedding .

Tips for a Successful Sleep Schedule

Watch for tired cues: Yawning, eye-rubbing, and fussiness are signs it&#x27;s time for a nap.

Create a consistent routine: A short, calming pre-nap routine (like a story or lullaby) in a dark, quiet room helps signal that it&#x27;s time to sleep .

Put your baby down drowsy but awake: This helps them learn to self-soothe .

Be flexible: If your baby is unwell or going through a developmental leap, their sleep needs may vary.

Trust your instincts. If you are worried about your baby&#x27;s sleep, call your health care provider right away.
