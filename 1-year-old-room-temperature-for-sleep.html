Ideal Room Temperature for Your 1-Year-Old&#x27;s Sleep

Creating a safe and comfortable sleep environment is important for your toddler&#x27;s health. One of the key factors to manage is the room temperature, especially since children at this age cannot regulate their body temperature as effectively as adults .

Why Room Temperature Matters

For children under 12 months, the primary concern is reducing the risk of Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS), where overheating is a known risk factor . Multiple official health sources, including the NHS and the HSE, specifically recommend a room temperature between 16°C and 20°C (60.8°F °F) for babies . This is supported by advice from the NIH, which suggests keeping the room at a comfortable temperature for an adult .

For toddlers over 12 months, the risk of SIDS decreases significantly. While some sources suggest a slightly broader range of 18°C (64°F) , the well-established safe range of 16°C remains a reliable guideline . To be safest, aim for the lower end of this range, around 18°C (65°F), as many authorities recommend this as the optimum temperature for young children .

How to Maintain a Safe Sleep Temperature

Here are practical ways to manage your child’s sleep environment:

Use a Room Thermometer: A simple, inexpensive room thermometer is the most reliable way to know the temperature of your child&#x27;s room, rather than relying on guesswork .

Dress Your Child Appropriately: Dress your child in a nappy, vest, and babygrow for sleep, adjusting layers according to the room temperature . Use a wearable blanket (sleep sack) with the appropriate TOG rating for the season. Never put a hat on your child indoors for sleep, as this can cause overheating .

Check for Signs of Overheating: Signs that your child is too hot include sweating, flushed or red skin, or their chest feeling hot to the touch .

Maintaining a consistently cool room will help your 1-year-old sleep more safely and soundly.
