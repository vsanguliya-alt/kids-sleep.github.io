About Kids Sleep Hub

Our Mission

Kids Sleep Hub provides evidence-based health guidance for parents of children aged 0-5. We synthesize official medical guidelines from NIH, MedlinePlus, AAP, and NHS into clear, actionable articles.

Sources We Trust

Every article is grounded in official public health guidelines:

  • NIH / MedlinePlus — U.S. National Institutes of Health
  • NHS.uk — UK National Health Service
  • AAP — American Academy of Pediatrics
  • CDC — Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

Editorial Process

  1. Research: We extract key facts from official guidelines.
  2. Generation: Content is drafted using AI assistance.
  3. Fact-check: All medical claims are cross-referenced with primary sources.
  4. Disclaimer: Content is for informational purposes only — not medical advice.

Important Disclaimer

This content is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your pediatrician or other qualified health provider with any questions regarding a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice because of something you have read on this site.
