# About Us This site provides evidence-based health information for parents of children aged 0-5. Our content is generated from public health guidelines published by authoritative sources: the U.S. National Institutes of Health (NIH), the UK's NICE, the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), and the World Health Organization (WHO). We are not a substitute for professional medical care. Every article links to its source guidelines so readers can verify claims. Our mission: make trustworthy pediatric guidance accessible and free.
