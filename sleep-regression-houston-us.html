Sleep Regression in Children: A Houston Parent's Guide

If your child, who was once sleeping through the night, is suddenly waking up frequently or fighting bedtime, you may be dealing with a sleep regression. This is a temporary phase where a child's sleep patterns become disrupted. The good news is that it's a normal part of development and typically lasts only a few weeks

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I call a doctor about regression?

If your child has difficulty breathing, persistent high fever, lethargy, or symptoms lasting beyond expected, contact a healthcare provider.

Is regression normal at this age?

Many variations are normal. Red-flag signs (listed above) mean you should seek care.

What helps at home?

Hydration, rest, and age-appropriate comfort measures. Always follow NHS/AAP guidance for medications.

How long does it last?

Most childhood episodes resolve within a few days. Persistent or worsening symptoms need evaluation.
