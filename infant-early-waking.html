Infant Early Waking: Why It Happens and What You Can Do

Waking up before 6 a.m. is one of the most common and stubborn sleep challenges parents face with babies . While it’s normal for babies to wake between 6 and 7:30 a.m., anything earlier is considered a night waking—and can usually be adjusted . Here’s an evidence-based look at why it happens and how to gently shift your baby’s wake-up time.
