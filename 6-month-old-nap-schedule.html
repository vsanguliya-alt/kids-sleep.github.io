6-Month-Old Nap Schedule: A Guide for Parents

At 6 months old, your baby&#x27;s sleep is becoming more predictable, making it an ideal time to establish a consistent nap schedule. Your baby&#x27;s circadian rhythms are firming up, which means they can better distinguish day from night and are capable of longer, more organized sleep .

How Much Sleep Does a 6-Month-Old Need?

A 6-month-old typically needs about 12–16 hours of sleep per day, including nighttime sleep and daytime naps . This usually breaks down to about 11hours of sleep at night and 2.5–3.5 hours of daytime sleep . Many babies at this age can sleep for a stretch of at least 6 hours at night .

6-Month-Old Nap Schedule: Sample Timetable

At 6 months, most babies still take 3 naps per day, though some may begin transitioning to 2 naps . Wake windows—the time your baby can comfortably stay awake between sleeps—are typically about 2 to 2.5 hours .

3-Nap Schedule (Most Common)

1st nap: Starts 2.5 hours after wake-up; typically lasts 60minutes

2nd nap: Starts 2.5 hours after the 1st nap ends; typically lasts 60minutes

3rd nap (catnap): Starts 2.5 hours after the 2nd nap; usually 30minutes, ending by late afternoon Transitioning to 2 Naps

Between 6 and 9 months, many babies drop their third nap . Signs your baby may be ready include consistently resisting the third nap or taking a long time to fall asleep at night . If you&#x27;re seeing these signs, you can try a schedule with two longer naps—one in the mid-morning and one in the early afternoon.

Tips for Successful Naps

Watch for sleepy cues: Look for signs like yawning, eye-rubbing, or fussiness, and put your baby down for a nap when they first appear tired .

Create a consistent routine: A short pre-nap routine (like a quick story or lullaby) in a dark, quiet room can help signal that it&#x27;s time to sleep .

Cap late afternoon naps: Avoid letting your baby sleep past 4:30–5:00 PM, as this can interfere with bedtime .
